Metamorphosis

One life told by hair alone, in one shot at a window: pigtails, a ponytail, a wedding updo, a tired bob, long silver hair, and a grandchild in the same pigtails. Her face does not age; the hair, the light, and the view carry the time. The film has music and no dialogue, and it runs for 90 seconds.

All hair in the film comes from HairDeck. Each style changes into the next in the latent space of HairDeck's card VAE.

Source and notes on GitHub. Backdrops, reference images, and music were generated with AI. Companion film: Regrowth.
